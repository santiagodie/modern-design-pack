# Final CTA

Reference for `modern-design`, step 3. `design-review` may also read it to check this component.

## Job

Catch the visitors who read everything and are now ready, and give the page a strong ending.

## When it applies

Long pages that persuade. Short pages for visitors who arrive ready to act may end with the main action already in view. Pages people read can end with the next thing to read instead.

## Content

- Restate the core promise in new words; don't repeat the hero headline verbatim.
- Repeat the offer and its key terms (trial length, price, guarantee, deadline for orders).
- One primary CTA, the same action as the hero. Optionally one lower-commitment action ("Talk to us").
- Risk reversal near the button ("Cancel anytime", "Free returns").
- Optionally one short real testimonial or proof line.
- **No new information.** A new feature here raises a new question at the worst moment.
- When the page has a FAQ, put the final CTA after it: the FAQ is where the last doubts get answered.
- When the offer has a real deadline, say so honestly. Never invent urgency.

## Layout per device

| | Phone | Tablet | Desktop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band | Full width, contrasting background | Same | Same |
| Content | Left-aligned or centered, full-width button in thumb reach | Centered, max about 560–640 px | Centered, max about 640–720 px |
| Padding | 48–80 px | 64–96 px | 96–160 px |
| Headline | 28–36 px | 32–44 px | 36–56 px |
| Sticky CTA | Hides when this section is visible | Same | — |

**Background:** use a contrasting band from the palette (a dark band on a light page, or the brand color). Use a gradient only when the brand itself uses one; gradient bands as decoration read as generic (checklist CO2).

## States and interaction

Same button states as `cta.md`: gated hover, immediate `:active`, visible focus, loading that keeps the label.

## Accessibility

- The section has a heading in the page outline (usually `h2`).
- The button label makes sense out of context ("Get a quote", not "Go").
- Text and button keep contrast on the band's color.

## Critical errors

- No final CTA: the page just ends in the footer.
- A different offer or destination from the hero.

## Common errors

- "Ready to get started?" as the headline.
- The final CTA placed before the FAQ.
- A new feature or claim introduced here.

## Level-up

- A benefit-recap headline built only from facts the user supplied (for a language school that confirmed both: "Speak at work in six months. Classes after 18:00.").
- Faces of a few real customers or the team.
- Measure clicks on this CTA separately: it often converts the most qualified visitors.

## Generic-pattern watch

CY1 (headline that fits any product), CO2 (gradient bands), CO3 (glow), LA6 (competing actions), CY4 (hype tone).

## Style notes

The final band is where the direction can be loudest: a full color field, a large type moment, a strong photo. Keep the action the clearest thing on it.
